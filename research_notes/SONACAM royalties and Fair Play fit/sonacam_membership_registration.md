# SONACAM: organisation, membership and work registration (as of Nov 2026)

Research date: 2026-11-24. Many key sources (Cameroon Tribune) are paywalled; only lead paragraphs were readable. Two primary-ish sources were unreachable (Music In Africa directory entry, Mimi Mefo Info article: connection reset).

## 1. Creation, legal status, governance, leadership, offices

### Takeaway
SONACAM was founded at a constitutive general assembly on 9 Sept 2017 and approved (agrément) by MINAC on 21 Dec 2017. It replaced the CMC/SOCAM disputes. Dr Francis Ateh Bazore (also written "Bazor") has chaired the board (PCA) since Dec 2020 and was re-elected on 30 Apr 2026. The last DG I could confirm by name is Jacques Gabriel Serge Amougou, installed in Jan 2021. His 2026 status is unconfirmed.

### Cited Findings
- Constitutive general assembly on 9 September 2017 at the Palais des congrès in Yaoundé. The source says artists reached agreement after more than a decade of turmoil, with strong government backing. Minister Narcisse Mouelle Kombi (MINAC) urged artists to end their disputes. SONACAM is classed in "catégorie B" (musical art) of copyright. — [Saimondy, 8 Oct 2017](https://www.saimondy.com/2017/10/08/sonacam-artistes-camerounais/)
- Official site states: non-profit civil society created in 2017, approved by ministerial decision N°0012/MINAC of 21 December 2017, sole purpose collective management of copyright in musical art, 99-year term, constitutive AG on 9 Sept 2017. — [sonacam.org](https://www.sonacam.org)
- The accreditation was signed on 21 December 2017 by Minister Narcisse Mouelle Kombi. It covers category B, musical art, a sector described as "under the rocks for almost a decade". — [IFACCA, 22 Dec 2017](https://www.ifacca.org/news/2017/12/22/cameroons-authors-rights-sonacam-gets-accreditatio/)
- Search-result summaries describe SONACAM as formed from the merger/succession of CMC (Cameroon Music Corporation) and SOCAM (Société civile camerounaise de l'art musical). — [Music In Africa directory entry](https://musicinafrica.net/node/25850) (page not fetchable; snippet only, treat as lower confidence)
- MINAC withdrew CMC's agrément in May 2008. In 2015 the Prime Minister annulled MINAC's agrément for "Socacim" (a music CMO), citing Article 75 of Law 2000/011 (one society per category). A 2015 amendment means agrément is granted, refused, suspended or withdrawn by MINAC "après visa du Premier ministre". — [Camer.be, PM et droit d'auteur de l'art musical](https://www.camer.be/amp/42355/11:1/cameroun-le-pm-donne-le-tempo-sur-le-droit-dauteur-de-lart-musical-cameroon.html)
- A Prime Ministerial decree excludes dual nationals from leading CMOs: board members must be Cameroonian. — [Camer.be](https://www.camer.be/amp/54869/1:6/cameroun-gestion-des-societes-des-droits-dauteurs-le-pm-exclut-la-double-nationalite-pour-les-dirigeants-cameroon.html)
- Legal base: Law No. 2000/011 of 19 Dec 2000 on copyright and neighbouring rights, and Decree No. 2001/956/PM of 1 Nov 2001 (implementing rules). — [OAPI copy of law](https://oapi.int/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Loi_19decembre_2000Cameroun.pdf); [WIPO Lex](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/fr/legislation/details/7757)
- **First PCA:** musician Sam Fan Thomas was elected president in 2017. — [Music In Africa: "Sam Fan Thomas élu président de la Sonacam"](https://musicinafrica.net/node/25830) (title only)
- **PCA since 2020:** on 12 Dec 2020 in Yaoundé, Ateh Bazore was elected PCA with 254 votes, ahead of Jean Pierre Essome (185), Messi Ambroise (85) and Wax Dey (69). He succeeded Sam Fan Thomas for a five-year term. Reports call him the first anglophone musician in the post. — [Cameroon Tribune 37060](https://cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/37060/fr.html/details_2); [Cameroun24](https://cameroun24.net/article/56198-urgent-ateh-bazore-vient-d-etre-elu-president-du-conseil-d-administration-de-la-sonacam.html); [Afrik.com](https://www.afrik.com/cameroun-ateh-bazore-elu-president-du-conseil-d-administration-de-la-sonacam)
- **DG (2021):** on about 27 Jan 2021, PCA Bazore installed Jacques Gabriel Serge Amougou as Directeur général, with Thérèse Eloumba and Edvige Sorel Kenembeni as directors of financial and legal affairs. The article says this restarted activities that had been suspended because of a long vacancy. Amougou was formerly a regional delegate of a predecessor society (text truncated). His stated priorities were health insurance for artists, a retirement pension ("SECU" project) and traceability. — [Cameroon Tribune 38229, 28 Jan 2021](https://www.cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/38229/fr.html/droits-dauteur-de-lart-musical-le-dg-de-la-sonacam-en-poste)
- In Oct 2025 a communiqué from "le Directeur Général" announced the 30 Sept 2025 distribution, but the press report does not name the DG. — [Cameroun24 70951](https://cameroun24.net/article/70951-sonacam-debut-des-paiements-de-la-r-partition-du-30-septembre-chaque-artiste-recevra-27-000-fcfa.html)
- **2022 governance crisis:** an interim order of the TGI Mfoundi (30 May 2022), in a case brought by the association ADANG against MINAC, was used to name five provisional administrators chaired by Esso Essomba (ex-PCA of Socinada). On 1 July 2022 MINAC warned against any "hostile takeover". The ministry and SONACAM's board appealed to the Cour d'appel du Centre, with a hearing set for 7 July 2022. The outcome is not in the readable text, but Bazore remained PCA afterwards. — [Cameroon Tribune 49423, 5 Jul 2022](https://www.cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/49423/en.html/droits-dauteur-la-nouvelle-affaire-sonacam)
- **Statute revision:** a combined ordinary and extraordinary AG with more than 400 members (about 28 Feb 2023, Yaoundé) revised the statutes and rules. The changes give managers more latitude and the power to exclude members or refer them to a disciplinary council. The board is to shrink from 15 to 11 members on the minister's recommendation. — [Cameroon Tribune 55504, 1 Mar 2023](https://cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/55504/fr.html/details_2)
- **2026 renewal:** officers' mandates expired in Dec 2025 and the bodies were renewed on 30 Apr 2026. — [Cameroon Tribune 76553, 8 May 2026](https://www.cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/76553/en.html/details_2)
- Bazore was re-elected PCA with 733 votes to 45 for Messi Ambroise (more than 94%), voted by the "artistes sociétaires". — [allAfrica / Camer.be, 1 May 2026](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202605010511.html); [Cameroon Tribune 76344](https://www.cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/76344/en.html/details_2); [Cameroon Tribune 76338](https://cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/76338/fr.html/details_2)
- **HQ:** "Shell Ahala", Center Park, Yaoundé. Phone +237 698 54 23 92. The emails listed are inconsistent (infos@onacam.cm in the header, info@sonacam.org in the footer). — [sonacam.org](https://www.sonacam.org)
- A Douala office is cited opposite Collège King Akwa (reader comment from 2019). — [Saimondy](https://www.saimondy.com/2017/10/08/sonacam-artistes-camerounais/)
- Regional delegations exist: the 2025 communiqué says mandate numbers are available "au siège, dans les délégations régionales et dans les foras d'artistes". — [Cameroun24 70951](https://cameroun24.net/article/70951-sonacam-debut-des-paiements-de-la-r-partition-du-30-septembre-chaque-artiste-recevra-27-000-fcfa.html)

### Inferences
- The official web domain appears to be sonacam.org. I found no evidence of a sonacam.cm site. A 2020 communiqué reportedly pointed members to www.sonacam.com (seen in a search summary only), so the domain may have changed.
- Governance is two-tier: an elected board (PCA, cut from 15 to 11 seats in 2023) and an appointed DG with directorates. MINAC is the supervising authority, together with a Commission de contrôle des organismes de gestion collective.

### Gaps
- Current DG name in 2025–2026 not confirmed. Amougou is the last named DG I found (2021).
- Staff numbers and the full list of regional delegations: no source found.
- The exact act merging or replacing CMC/SOCAM is not confirmed from a readable primary source.

## 2. Becoming a member: categories, fees, documents, rights managed

### Takeaway
SONACAM manages authors' rights (droit d'auteur) only, for authors, composers and music publishers. Membership is in person: a paper file (ID, photos, CD of original works, form) is reviewed by an identification/admissions commission, and the reported fee is 10,000 FCFA. Neighbouring rights for performers and producers are outside SONACAM. A neighbouring-rights society (SCDV) was being founded in 2017, but I found no evidence that it is accredited or operating.

### Cited Findings
- Eligible categories are authors (lyricists), composers, author-composers and music publishers. Conditions are Cameroonian nationality or residence, plus original works.
- Required documents: CNI photocopy, four 4x4 photos, a CD with 5–6 original tracks, the membership form (available at HQ) and an artistic CV.
- Steps: submit the file at Yaoundé HQ or the Douala branch, then an admissions commission listens to the CD to check originality and authorship. After acceptance the applicant pays the 10,000 FCFA fee and receives a member card. All existing and future works must then be declared.
- This page is a secondary blog (France-based, 2026) and cites no primary source for these figures. — [Muzisecur blog](https://www.muzisecur.fr/blog/sonacam-adhesion-procedure-cameroun-auteur-compositeur/)
- An older account (2019 reader exchange) gives a similar list: a 4x4 photo, receipts from former societies, a CNI photocopy and the CD/album covers, submitted at a regional agency. It describes the 10,000 FCFA registration fee as "rétention à la source" (deducted from royalties rather than paid upfront). — [Saimondy comments](https://www.saimondy.com/2017/10/08/sonacam-artistes-camerounais/)
  - Conflict: Muzisecur says the fee is paid after acceptance, while the 2019 comment says it is withheld at source. Both may have applied at different times.
- In June 2023 there were about 1,000 additional artists whose works had "not yet passed the identification commission". This confirms a commission-based vetting step. — [Cameroon Tribune 58312, 23 Jun 2023](https://www.cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/58312/fr.html/droits-dauteur-dans-lart-musical-notes-despoir)
- The "artistes sociétaires" vote at the general assembly (2026). — [allAfrica](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202605010511.html)
- SONACAM's official site invites creators to join but gives no step-by-step requirements. It mentions that paper membership forms are slated for digitisation. — [sonacam.org](https://www.sonacam.org)
- Other Cameroonian CMOs by category: SOCILADRA (literature), SCAAP (audiovisual/photography) and SOCADAP (visual arts). Joining a CMO and declaring works creates a dated official record. — [Lefisk.cm](https://lefisk.cm/blog/droit-auteur-cameroun-loi-2000-duree-protection-sonacam); see also [Cameroon Tribune 55773 "Sociladra, Socadap, Sonacam"](https://cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/55773/fr.html/details_2) (title only)
- Neighbouring rights: the Société camerounaise des droits voisins (SCDV) held founding meetings in July 2017 and was then seeking MINAC accreditation. — [Camer.be search summary](https://www.camer.be/41916/1:6/musique) (low confidence; not read in full)
- Cameroon's WIPO Copyright Treaty accession entered into force 9 Apr 2025. — [WIPO country profile](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/country-profiles/cm). A May 2026 article says Cameroon ratified both 1996 WIPO treaties (WCT and WPPT). — [Cameroon Tribune 76557](https://cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/76557/fr.html/diffusion-il-faut-ratisser-large)

### Inferences
- Performers and producers who are not also authors or composers probably cannot collect neighbouring rights through SONACAM. Publishers are admitted.
- The formal categories "adhérent" and "sociétaire" were not explained in any source. Only the word "sociétaires" (voting members) appears.

### Gaps
- No official SONACAM fee schedule, form or statutes text found online. The 10,000 FCFA fee should be confirmed with SONACAM.
- Whether SCDV (or any neighbouring-rights CMO) is accredited and operating in 2026 is unknown.
- How long admission takes.

## 3. Declaring and registering works; databases, identifiers, CISAC, WIPO Connect

### Takeaway
Work declaration is still mainly physical: CDs are submitted and checked by commission, with paper forms. SONACAM signed for WIPO Connect on 7 Jul 2021, and the PCA says it is in use, with about 105,000 works documented in a physical and digital database. The official site has "Espace Créateurs" and "Déclarer une œuvre" sections, but that page returned 404. A member portal showing works and broadcasts is listed as a future project. I found no confirmation of CISAC membership, ISWC/IPI assignment, or a mobile app.

### Cited Findings
- The site advertises declaring musical works for creators and publishers with tracking of rights and payments. It has an "Espace Créateurs" (creators/publishers) and an "Espace Utilisateurs" (users, with an online quote/licence-pricing page, "Obtenir un devis"). The WIPO agreement for WIPO Connect was signed on 7 July 2021, and WIPO Connect is presented as the system for documentation, licensing and distribution. A member portal showing registered works and detected broadcasts is listed as a future project. — [sonacam.org](https://www.sonacam.org); [Obtenir un devis](https://www.sonacam.org/obtenir-un-devis)
- The "Déclarer une œuvre" URL (sonacam.org/declarer-une-oeuvre) returned HTTP 404 on 2026-11-24. — [attempted URL](https://www.sonacam.org/declarer-une-oeuvre)
- In May 2026 the PCA said WIPO Connect is used. He said about 105,000 musical works are documented in a physical and digital database, the documentation service was reorganised, and digital professional cards were introduced for artists. — [Cameroon Tribune 76344, May 2026](https://www.cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/76344/en.html/details_2)
- A Feb 2023 AG article mentions a WIPO partnership (text cut off). — [Cameroon Tribune 55504](https://cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/55504/fr.html/details_2)
- Membership requires a CD of 5–6 original tracks, which the commission listens to. After joining, all existing and future works must be declared, but the method is not detailed. — [Muzisecur](https://www.muzisecur.fr/blog/sonacam-adhesion-procedure-cameroun-auteur-compositeur/)
- In May 2026 Cameroon Tribune said SONACAM must adopt the best digital tools to capture online and streaming income and speed up its "dossier de perception en ligne". It also said SONACAM could sign bilateral or multilateral reciprocity agreements with sister CMOs. These are presented as goals, not achievements. — [Cameroon Tribune 76557, 8 May 2026](https://cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/76557/fr.html/diffusion-il-faut-ratisser-large)
- Digitisation of collection was named a priority of the new mandate, and observers demand "accessible data for artists". — [allAfrica, 1 May 2026](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202605010511.html)
- **CISAC:** CMC held Cameroon's CISAC seat and lost it after its 2008 de-accreditation. Cameroon Tribune reported that SONACAM's CISAC membership file was expected to conclude "dans les prochaines semaines" (article undated in results). — [Cameroon Tribune 58771](https://cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/58771/fr.html/details_2); [Cameroon Tribune 76556](https://cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/76556/fr.html/details_2)
- A SACEM partnership (5 years) and WIPO cooperation are mentioned without documentation. — [Muzisecur](https://www.muzisecur.fr/blog/sonacam-adhesion-procedure-cameroun-auteur-compositeur/)

### Inferences
- WIPO Connect is built around CISAC-standard identifiers (IPI/ISWC), so SONACAM's use of it suggests a move toward them. I found no evidence that SONACAM members actually receive IPI numbers or that works get ISWCs. Members do get a "numéro de mandat", the internal ID used for payouts.
- Deposit by CD remains the norm in reports. I found no evidence of MP3 or online upload being accepted.

### Gaps
- CISAC membership status in 2026 is not confirmed. The CISAC member directory was not checked successfully.
- ISWC, ISRC and IPI use: no source.
- No mobile app or "e-SONACAM" product found.
- No WIPO page confirming a SONACAM WIPO Connect deployment was found. WIPO searches returned only general Cameroon activity.

## 4. How members are paid

### Takeaway
Distributions are irregular, lump-sum events announced by communiqué. Members collect cash at Express Union Finance agencies by showing their ID and SONACAM mandate number. Amounts per artist are small: a flat 27,000 FCFA "advance" in Oct 2025, and 25,000–300,000 FCFA in 2023. The board promises quarterly payments for 2026–2031.

### Cited Findings
- The 30 Sept 2025 distribution was paid from 7 Oct 2025 at all Express Union Finance SA agencies nationwide. Members had to present ID and their mandate number (available at HQ, regional delegations and artist forums). The envelope was 40 million FCFA, and the board fixed a flat 27,000 FCFA per beneficiary as an "avance sur droits d'auteur". The DG asked artists for patience. — [Cameroun24 70951](https://cameroun24.net/article/70951-sonacam-debut-des-paiements-de-la-r-partition-du-30-septembre-chaque-artiste-recevra-27-000-fcfa.html)
- The 27,000 FCFA payout angered artists. — [Mimi Mefo Info](https://mimimefoinfos.com/la-sonacam-indigne-les-artistes-camerounais-avec-27-000-fcfa-davance-de-droits-dauteur/) (headline only; page not fetchable)
- June 2023 was the fourth distribution: 80 million FCFA to 2,040 artists, 25,000–300,000 FCFA each, paid after the Fête de la musique. The Jan 2023 distribution reached 1,700 artists, and the 2022 distribution paid 110 million FCFA to 1,500 artists. About 40 artists received social aid. — [Cameroon Tribune 58312](https://www.cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/58312/fr.html/droits-dauteur-dans-lart-musical-notes-despoir)
- A secondary source gives 112 million FCFA distributed in 2021, 340 million FCFA of CMC/SOCAM arrears redistributed in 2022, and an average payout of about 25,000 FCFA. — [Muzisecur](https://www.muzisecur.fr/blog/sonacam-adhesion-procedure-cameroun-auteur-compositeur/)
  - Possible conflict or duplication: 110M (2022, Cameroon Tribune) vs 112M (2021, Muzisecur).
- The PCA claims nine payments in five years (2021–2026), against two by the previous team. Planned for 2026–2031: quarterly royalty payments, health insurance, low-interest housing loans via Crédit Foncier du Cameroun, and "Music Palaces". Revenue includes a 12 FCFA per crate beer levy agreed with brewers, and fees for government use of music are planned. — [Cameroon Tribune 76344](https://www.cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/76344/en.html/details_2)
- Planned collection measures (2023): a joint MINAC/Commerce decree to collect at source from drinks outlets, and recovery from mobile operators. — [Cameroon Tribune 58312](https://www.cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/58312/fr.html/droits-dauteur-dans-lart-musical-notes-despoir)
- The site claims 80% is redistributed to artists and makes a "24h" sharing claim, both unexplained marketing figures. — [sonacam.org](https://www.sonacam.org)
- A 2020 communiqué on 2019 advances invited members to check beneficiary lists on the website. — [Cameroun24 55128](https://cameroun24.net/article-amp/55128-les-artistes-affilies-a-la-sonacam-passent-a-la-caisse-ce-jour.html) (search summary)
- Over 80% of creators are said to live below the poverty line, and recurring problems include low collection from heavy users and suspected mismanagement. — [Cameroon Tribune 76553](https://www.cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/76553/en.html/details_2)

### Inferences
- No evidence of bank transfer or Mobile Money payouts, or of per-work itemised statements. Payment appears to be over the counter via Express Union, keyed to a mandate number. Flat payments such as the 27,000 FCFA advance imply that distribution is not usage-based.
- Dividing the 40M FCFA envelope by 27,000 FCFA gives at most about 1,480 beneficiaries (my calculation, not reported).

### Gaps
- Statement format (relevé de droits), whether individual statements exist, and the distribution rules or key (radio/TV/live split) were not found.
- Mobile Money payouts: no source.

## 5. Membership and repertoire size

### Takeaway
About 6,700 members (June 2023) rising to 7,734 registered members (May 2026), with about 105,000 documented works (May 2026, PCA's claim). The 2026 AG cast 778 votes.

### Cited Findings
- About 6,700 affiliated artists, plus about 1,000 pending identification (June 2023). — [Cameroon Tribune 58312](https://www.cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/58312/fr.html/droits-dauteur-dans-lart-musical-notes-despoir)
- 7,734 registered members and about 105,000 works documented (May 2026). — [Cameroon Tribune 76344](https://www.cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/76344/en.html/details_2)
- 733 + 45 = 778 votes cast at the Apr 2026 AG. — [allAfrica](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202605010511.html)
- More than 400 members attended the Feb 2023 AG. — [Cameroon Tribune 55504](https://cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/55504/fr.html/details_2)

### Inferences
- Only about 1,500–2,000 members receive any payment per distribution, roughly 20–30% of the membership.

### Gaps
- No audited annual report or published collection totals found. The repertoire figure is a claim by the PCA during his campaign or mandate, not independently verified.
