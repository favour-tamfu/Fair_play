# SONACAM royalty collection channels in Cameroon, with focus on the brewery / beer levy (state as of Nov 2026)

Note on sources: almost everything below comes from Cameroonian press. I could not find the official text of the 2023 joint decision, the 2025 protocol or any SONACAM or CCOGC annual report online. Several Cameroon Tribune articles are paywalled, so only their opening parts could be read. Dates are the ones printed in the articles.

## 1. Is there a beer / brewery levy linked to copyright royalties?

### Takeaway
YES. The "beer levy" is real. It is a copyright royalty collected at source on drinks sold to bars and drinking establishments: **1 FCFA per bottle, or 12 FCFA per crate (casier)**. Large drink distributors withhold it in the distribution chain and pay it into a special deposit account for rights holders. Five collective management organisations (CMOs, French OGC) share it, and press reports say 4% goes to a MINAC culture fund. Its legal basis is **joint decision n°00034/MINAC/MINCOMMERCE of 25 April 2023**. A protocol with **Boissons du Cameroun / SABC** was signed on **6 October 2025**. A convention with the CMOs and the bartenders' union was signed on **13 February 2026**, when the scheme formally entered into application. This is a substitute for the old flat royalty charged to each bar. It is **not** a tax: it is not a share of excise duty or of any beverage tax.

### Cited Findings
**Legal basis and mechanism**
- The reform rests on joint decision no. 00034/MINAC/MINCOMMERCE of 25 April 2023. The fee is collected at source directly from large beverage distributors, not from bars, and transferred to a special account for rights holders (ayants droit). The rate is 12 FCFA per crate, which the article equates to 1 franc per bottle. Its entry into application was made official at a press conference in Yaoundé on 13 Feb 2026, attended by SONACAM, other CMOs, bar-owner unions, beverage distributors, state representatives and artists. SONACAM board chair Dr Ateh Bazore called it a "partenariat gagnant-gagnant". The article names no specific brewer. — [Relais 237, 14 Feb 2026](https://relais237.com/revolution-dans-le-droit-dauteur-au-cameroun-la-redevance-a-la-source-met-fin-aux-contentieux)
- Convention signed on 13 Feb 2026 in Yaoundé by **SONACAM** (music), **SCDV** (Société camerounaise des droits voisins, neighbouring rights), **SCAAP** (audiovisual and photographic arts), **SOCADAP** (visual and graphic arts) and **SYNDEBARCAM** (Syndicat des Barmans du Cameroun, a union, not a CMO). It covers bars and drinking establishments such as cabarets, snack-bars and lounges. Royalties are deducted at source at 1 FCFA per bottle of beer, or 12 FCFA per crate. The article adds that the rate depends on the establishment's category, a detail that is not explained. Ateh Bazor said payments to rights holders will be made four times a year. The Commission de contrôle des organismes de gestion collective (CCOGC), headed by Pr François Edimo, monitors the scheme. The money goes into a special deposit account and is then redistributed. — [Le Monde Professionnel, 17 Feb 2026](https://mondeprofessionnel.press/prestations-dans-les-debits-de-boisson-les-droits-dauteurs-des-artistes-dorenavant-preleves-a-la-source/)
- **Protocol of 6 October 2025** between **Boissons du Cameroun**, SONACAM and several bartenders' associations. The levy is a direct deduction in the distribution chain: 1 FCFA per bottle, 12 FCFA per crate. The funds go to **5 CMOs**, and **4% goes to the MINAC cultural fund**. The article names neither the five CMOs nor the entity that physically remits the money. — [Glob'Média, 11 Aug 2026](https://globmedia.online/2026/08/11/bras-de-fer-entre-les-barmans-et-la-sonacam-12-francs-cfa-de-la-discorde/)
- Cameroon Tribune (28 Apr 2026) reports a MINAC meeting on CMO performance. A contract with the "Société anonyme des Boissons du Cameroun (SABC)" made it possible to apply the joint MINAC–Mincommerce decision, so that royalties owed by bars and drinking establishments are withheld at source. CCOGC president Edimo reported four distributions between April 2025 and April 2026, with "montants de plus en plus consistants". No figures are visible before the paywall. — [Cameroon Tribune, 28 Apr 2026](https://www.cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/76231/fr.html/organismes-de-gestion-collective-du-droit-dauteur-on-evalue-les-performances)

**Money amounts from the levy**
- In **July 2026**, SONACAM president Francis Ateh Bazore announced the recovery of **100 million FCFA** collected at source through the 12 FCFA/crate levy. He promised a distribution "dans les semaines à venir", with payouts of 30,000 to 300,000 FCFA per rights holder. On 22 Sept 2026, artist Henri Lotin (son of Eboa Lotin) posted a photo of **2,000 FCFA** received for his July 2026 rights, which caused public anger. The article does not confirm whether the 100M has been distributed in full. — [Camer.be, 22 Sept 2026](https://www.camer.be/94843/1:6/cameroun-eboa-lotin-2-000-fcfa-de-droits-dauteur-la-colere.html); also [allAfrica](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202609230156.html)
- A Panorama Papers headline says the "1 franc par bouteille" reform "rapportera des milliards de FCFA aux artistes". This is a projection, and the page could not be fetched (HTTP 400). — [Panorama Papers](https://panoramapapers.com/cameroun-un-franc-par-bouteille-la-reforme-qui-rapportera-des-milliards-de-fcfa-aux-artistes/)

**Conflict with bar operators (2026)**
- On **6 August 2026**, many bars closed for a **three-day strike** called by SYNAPOSOCAM (Syndicat national des propriétaires et exploitants des sociétés commerciales du Cameroun, president Joseph Marie Anguissa Anguissa). The union says it supports paying artists but objects that the 12 FCFA levy was imposed without consultation, and that most field operators were ignored when the protocol was agreed. It wants new negotiations. SONACAM replied in a statement of 10 Aug 2026 that the automated collection protects tenanciers: it ends unannounced inspections, seizures of sound equipment and forced closures, simplifies financial tracking and gives creators a stable income. — [Glob'Média, 11 Aug 2026](https://globmedia.online/2026/08/11/bras-de-fer-entre-les-barmans-et-la-sonacam-12-francs-cfa-de-la-discorde/)

**Earlier history: brewers as large direct users and debtors**
- 27 Apr 2022: after a consultation with the CCOGC and CMO boards, MINAC (Minister Bidoung Mkpatt) announced that CMOs claimed about **1 billion FCFA** of arrears from before 2018: **SABC 600M**, **Guinness Cameroun 301M**, **SNH 100M**. SABC placed the money in a separate account until disputes over which CMO was entitled to collect were resolved. — [Investir au Cameroun, 27 Apr 2022](https://www.investiraucameroun.com/economie/2704-17837-droits-d-auteurs-un-milliard-de-fcfa-reclames-a-la-sabc-guinness-cameroun-et-la-snh)
- Ecomatin: SABC had paid about 600M FCFA through dialogue with the recovery commission, and reportedly close to 2 billion over 17 years. Guinness offered 300M to settle a debt of more than 15 years, which artists called derisory. The commission also approached UCB. — [Ecomatin](https://ecomatin.net/desaccord-entre-guinness-cameroun-et-les-societes-de-gestion-collective-de-droits-dauteur) (date not visible)
- July 2021 background: about **25 billion FCFA** of arrears was owed by some thirty companies and users. Three brewing companies alone owed **16 billion FCFA** for 2005–2017. These are claimed figures. — [StopBlaBlaCam, 7 Apr 2025](https://www.stopblablacam.com/societe/0704-14112-droits-d-auteur-la-crtv-annonce-le-paiement-de-850-millions-fcfa-d-arrieres-de-redevance-aux-artistes-en-greve)
- On 12 Nov 2021, artists demonstrated at the headquarters of UCB (Union Camerounaise des Brasseries), saying it had "not paid copyright for 20 years". — [Le Magazine du Manager](https://lemagazinedumanager.com/24940-les-droits-dauteurs-au-cameroun-divisent-artistes-et-brasseurs.html); [Investir au Cameroun](https://www.investiraucameroun.com/economie/1111-17125-les-droits-d-auteurs-au-c-ur-d-une-manifestation-des-artistes-contre-l-union-camerounaise-des-brasseries)
- A blogger reports that Guinness used artists' works for 15 years without paying. This is an opinion source. — [Saimondy, 29 Oct 2021](https://www.saimondy.com/2021/10/29/cameroun-15-ans-que-guinness-utilise-les-oeuvres-dartistes-sans-payer/)

### Inferences
- The "beer levy" is functionally a **collective licence for bars that is bundled into the price of drinks**. Distributors withhold 12 FCFA per crate on behalf of the bars. It replaces the earlier system of field agents, bailiffs and equipment seizures. Bars are probably still the economic payers through their wholesale prices, which is why the bar unions are angry.
- Rough scale check (my estimate, not sourced): 100M FCFA at 12 FCFA per crate is about 8.3M crates, or about 100M bottles. Cameroon's beer market is several billion bottles a year, so the 100M probably covers only part of a period or only part of the distributors. Annual potential at full coverage would be several billion FCFA. Treat this as speculative.
- The levy is shared among music (SONACAM), neighbouring rights (SCDV) and possibly other CMOs. No source gives the split key, so SONACAM does not receive 100% of it.
- It is **not** linked to excise duty (droits d'accises) or a beverage tax. I found no evidence of any share of excise duty going to copyright.

### Gaps
- No official text was found online for decision 00034/MINAC/MINCOMMERCE, the 6 Oct 2025 protocol or the 13 Feb 2026 convention. [MINAC's texts page](https://minac.gov.cm/fr/documentation/textes-et-lois/droit-dauteur-et-droits-voisins) may host it.
- The split among the 5 CMOs is unknown. The 4% MINAC share comes from a single source (Glob'Média), and the Cameroon Tribune article on the culture fund (CAS) does not list this 4% among the fund's revenues, which makes it uncertain.
- It is unclear whether Guinness Cameroun and UCB also apply the levy, or only Boissons du Cameroun / SABC. Sources mention "grands distributeurs" generically.
- Whether "1 FCFA per bottle" applies to all drinks or only beer is unclear (one source says "bouteille de bière", others say "boissons"). How "category of establishment" modulates the rate is also unclear.
- I found nothing on how the August 2026 strike ended, and no annual total for the levy.

## 2. How are bars, nightclubs, hotels, transport and events charged, and how is the money collected?

### Takeaway
Before 2026, bars and other public places were billed through flat annual fees set by a decision of 13 January 2006. In practice this meant field agents, bailiffs and seizures of equipment, and governors were asked to support collection in 2021. Since February 2026, drinking establishments are covered by the levy at source described in section 1. I found no current published SONACAM tariff grid for bars, hotels, transport or events.

### Cited Findings
- A flat annual fee schedule exists, set by the **Décision du 13 janvier 2006**. Ecomatin applies it to companies: **large enterprises (about 400) owe 16–32M FCFA/year each**, and **medium enterprises (over 11,000) owe 3.2–8M FCFA/year each**. On that basis it computes a theoretical potential of **40–100 billion FCFA/year**, while actual copyright collections "n'atteint guère 500 millions de FCFA par an depuis 2004". It counts 12,758 rights holders on the national file. — [Ecomatin, 25 Jun 2020](https://ecomatin.net/droit-dauteur-entre-40-et-100-milliards-de-fcfa-a-recouvrer)
- Before the levy at source, artists seized equipment (TVs, speakers, radios) from bars as compensation for unpaid royalties, which damaged relations. — [Le Monde Professionnel, Feb 2026](https://mondeprofessionnel.press/prestations-dans-les-debits-de-boisson-les-droits-dauteurs-des-artistes-dorenavant-preleves-a-la-source/). The earlier model relied on inspections, seizures and disputes with bailiffs (huissiers). — [Relais 237](https://relais237.com/revolution-dans-le-droit-dauteur-au-cameroun-la-redevance-a-la-source-met-fin-aux-contentieux)
- On 5 Aug 2021, MINAC sent a fax message to the 10 regional governors asking them to back SONACAM administrators in collecting royalties for 2021 ("Facilitation des perceptions des redevances dues au droit d'auteur par la Sonacam"). Breweries, supermarkets and mobile phone companies refused to pay, citing confusion over which CMO was legitimate. — [Saimondy, 8 Aug 2021](https://www.saimondy.com/2021/08/08/perceptions-du-droit-dauteur-de-la-sonacam-le-message-fax-du-minac-aux-gouverneurs/)
- Events are not charged through a CMO grid. MINAC charges a "cultural event authorisation" for festivals: 15,000 F for an individual, 50,000 F for a legal person or community, 500,000 F for a company, and 500,000 F to 1M F for large international concerts. There is also an "entertainment entrepreneur licence" for show venues, amount not given. These fees feed the culture fund (CAS), not artists' royalties. — [Cameroon Tribune, 30 Oct 2026](https://www.cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/82149/fr.html/details_2)
- CMOs license users, including bars, and collect fees under Law 2000/011. This is a general summary. — [Lefisk](https://lefisk.cm/blog/droit-auteur-cameroun-loi-2000-duree-protection-sonacam)

### Inferences
- The earlier per-establishment flat fee for bars has, at least for drinking establishments, been replaced in practice by the per-crate levy. Hotels, nightclubs that do not sell distributed drinks, transport and shops presumably remain on the per-user licence or flat fee model. This is unconfirmed.

### Gaps
- I could not find the current tariff grid (bars, cabarets, hotels, buses, shops), the 2006 decision's text, or any "guichet unique" or joint collection with municipalities.

## 3. Broadcasters, telecoms, private copying, the culture fund (CAS) and state subsidy

### Takeaway
CRTV is the only user consistently described as actually paying. It cleared 850M FCFA of 2015–2017 arrears between Dec 2024 and early 2025. Telecom operators (ringtones, ringback tones, streaming) are described as non-payers or under-payers, with no figures found. The private copying levy exists in law but has never been operational. The culture fund (Compte d'Affectation Spéciale, CAS) lost its 1bn FCFA state subsidy and collected only 114M in 2024 and 65M in 2025.

### Cited Findings
- **CRTV**: it owed **850M FCFA** for 2015–2017 to the five former CMOs (SOCILADRA, SCAAP, CMC, SOCAM, SOCADAP) under a settlement protocol of **4 Sept 2024**. Tranches: 318.75M paid Dec 2024, 318.75M due Dec 2024, and 212.5M due Feb 2025. CRTV says the debt is fully settled. Artists had struck in front of the Prime Minister's office on 2 Apr 2025. — [StopBlaBlaCam, 7 Apr 2025](https://www.stopblablacam.com/societe/0704-14112-droits-d-auteur-la-crtv-annonce-le-paiement-de-850-millions-fcfa-d-arrieres-de-redevance-aux-artistes-en-greve); [allAfrica, 8 Apr 2025](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202504080188.html). Another report says CRTV paid 350M. — [Africa-Press](https://www.africa-press.net/cameroun/economie/cest-fait-la-crtv-paye-enfin-les-350-millions-de-fcfa). The tranche figures conflict between sources.
- As of May 2026, only CRTV is described as genuinely paying. The article calls for SONACAM to revalue its agreements with mobile operators so that they pay for ringback tones, downloads and integrated streaming, and to bill other audiovisual media, web media and member companies of GECAM. — [Cameroon Tribune, 8 May 2026](https://www.cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/76554/fr.html/collecte-du-droit-dauteur-une-assiette-dusagers-elargir)
- **Private copying**: Article 11 of the implementing texts says the private copying levy on phonograms and videograms is set by order of the culture minister, and the split among CMOs by ministerial decision. For printed works (reprography), the rate is 5% of the sale price of reproduction devices (Art. 14). — [MINAC texts](https://minac.gov.cm/fr/documentation/textes-et-lois/droit-dauteur-et-droits-voisins) (via search excerpt). In Jan 2024 the levy was still not collected: Sam Mbende said the problem had lasted 34 years, that he had started steps in 2004, and that 2–3 implementing orders and Finance Ministry support were needed. The split is 25% authors, 25% performers and 25% producers, with the last quarter not stated. — [Camer.be, 28 Jan 2024](https://www.camer.be/actualites/societe/la-remuneration-de-la-copie-privee-au-cameroun-34-ans-deja-quest-ce-qui-coince-cameroon.html). In Oct 2026, private copying is still described as legally planned but not functioning. — [Cameroon Tribune, 30 Oct 2026](https://www.cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/82149/fr.html/details_2)
- **Culture fund (CAS)**: created by decree 2001/389 of 5 Dec 2001 — [Cameroun24](https://cameroun24.net/article/47281-pres-de-222-millions-du-compte-d-affectation-speciale-debloques.html). From 2001 to 2019 the state paid MINAC a direct subsidy of **1bn FCFA**. Under the SND30 strategy the fund must now raise its own non-tax revenue. Ceilings were 500M (2022) and 200M (2023). Actual collections were under 200M (2023), **114M (2024)** and **65M (2025)**. Some 2024 subsidies were paid from arrears ("restes à payer"). — [Cameroon Tribune, 30 Oct 2026](https://www.cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/82149/fr.html/details_2)

### Gaps
- I found no amounts for MTN or Orange, private radio and TV, or the CRTV annual flat fee. No CISAC or WIPO figure for Cameroon was retrieved (not searched in depth).

## 4. Totals collected and distributed per year, and the share from public places

### Takeaway
No audited annual totals are public. Collections have historically been in the hundreds of millions of FCFA a year, not billions, while the theoretical potential is 40–100bn. Recent distributions are small and contested. The beer levy (100M by July 2026) is now one of the largest single recurring channels alongside CRTV.

### Cited Findings
- Copyright collections were "barely 500M FCFA/year since 2004". — [Ecomatin, 2020](https://ecomatin.net/droit-dauteur-entre-40-et-100-milliards-de-fcfa-a-recouvrer)
- The SONACAM president said it pays about **350M FCFA/yr** to artists, or roughly 116,000 FCFA per member, out of about 3,000 members. — [Camer.be, Sept 2026](https://www.camer.be/94843/1:6/cameroun-eboa-lotin-2-000-fcfa-de-droits-dauteur-la-colere.html). Mbende cited "350 millions" collected to be divided among 4 CMOs, against about 20bn in Algeria. — [Camer.be interview](https://www.camer.be/culture/musique/cameroun-sam-mbende-on-ne-negocie-pas-les-instructions-du-chef-de-letat-cameroon.html) (via search excerpt)
- SONACAM collected **112M FCFA**. One source dates this to 2021, another to 2019–2020, so the date is uncertain. About 76% of it was distributed, to 1,497 artists. In 2023, 80M was distributed to 2,040 artists. — search excerpts from [237online](https://www.237online.com/repartition-des-droits-dauteurs-la-pomme-de-la-discorde/) and [Cameroon Tribune](https://www.cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/58312/fr.html/droits-dauteur-dans-lart-musical-notes-despoir) (not fetched in full; verify)
- **Arrears clearance (2005–2017)**: about **1.2bn FCFA** was recovered from users by the "Comité technique ad hoc CMC-SOCAM" (July 2022). — [Cameroon Tribune, 20 Jul 2022](https://cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/49833/fr.html/details_2). In August 2022, four CMOs distributed **340M FCFA** to 685 beneficiaries for works exploited in 2005–2009, with payments ranging from under 5,000 FCFA to 5M FCFA. — [Camer.be](https://www.camer.be/94843/1:6/cameroun-eboa-lotin-2-000-fcfa-de-droits-dauteur-la-colere.html); [Cameroon Tribune](https://cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/50537/fr.html/details_2)
- **Oct 2025**: SONACAM paid a flat **27,000 FCFA** advance per artist from its 30 Sept 2025 distribution (communiqué of 6 Oct 2025). — [SCN, 9 Oct 2025](https://sportsandculturenetwork.com/droits-dauteur-seulement-27-000-fcfa-par-artiste/). Camer.be says the total envelope was 40M FCFA. — [Camer.be](https://www.camer.be/94843/1:6/cameroun-eboa-lotin-2-000-fcfa-de-droits-dauteur-la-colere.html)

### Inferences
- If roughly 350–500M FCFA a year is the historical baseline, a beer levy that delivered 100M in its first months (Feb–Jul 2026) could already be 20–30% or more of SONACAM's yearly income. That would make bars, through drink distributors, plausibly the largest channel after CRTV. This is speculative, since no channel breakdown is published.

### Gaps
- There is no official per-channel breakdown and no SONACAM financial statement, and the CISAC figures for Cameroon were not verified. The CMO landscape changed (CMC and SOCAM were replaced by SONACAM around 2017–2018), which makes series before 2018 hard to compare.
