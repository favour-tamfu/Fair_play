# SONACAM royalty distribution (répartition) method and history of disputes (as of Nov 2026)

Research date: 24 Nov 2026. About 15 tool calls. Sources are mostly Cameroonian press. Several pages were paywalled (Cameroon Tribune) or failed to load (Mimi Mefo Info, Cameroon-Info.net). Those are flagged below.

## Q1. Which distribution rules and keys does SONACAM use, and on what usage data?

### Takeaway
No published SONACAM règlement de répartition with percentage keys (admin costs, social fund, author/composer/publisher split) turned up. In practice, recent distributions have been forfaits (flat sums: 25,000 FCFA in 2019, 27,000 FCFA in Oct 2025) or a tiered "grille" (25,000 to 300,000 FCFA). Eligibility and tiers reportedly depend on membership category, notoriety, presence in broadcasters' registers and holding a leadership role, not on systematic usage data. SONACAM's own website describes usage-based distribution and broadcast or digital monitoring as a plan, not as current practice.

### Cited Findings
- 2022 eligibility criteria: membership in a distribution reportedly depends on "presence on media-company broadcast registers, membership category (adherent, trainee, sociétaire, professional, honorary), notoriety, and any leadership position held" — [237online, 21 May 2022 (updated 6 Aug 2025)](https://www.237online.com/droit-dauteurs-la-sonacam-a-la-croisee-des-chemins/)
- June 2023: Board chair Francis Ateh Bazor(e) referred to a "grille de répartition" without describing its rules. He said it was "un peu difficile de dire avec précision qui mérite le plus" because of where the money came from. Payouts ran from 25,000 to 300,000 F — [Cameroon Tribune, 23 June 2023](https://cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/58312/fr.html/details_2)
- Oct 2025: the Board of Directors chose a single flat 27,000 FCFA per beneficiary. It called this an "avance sur droits d'auteur" and a transitional measure to distribute available funds quickly. Total envelope 40 million FCFA — [Cameroun24.net](https://cameroun24.net/article/70951-SONACAM___debut_des_paiements_de_la_repartition_du.html); [Camer.be via AllAfrica, 22 Sep 2026](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202609230156.html)
- Dec 2019: 25,000 XAF per artist to 1,416 artists (total 35 million XAF, per then-chair Sam Fan Thomas). A SONACAM delegate described it as encouragement "ahead of the actual distribution of copyrights royalties". A MINAC official said payments should instead follow "how much of his or her work was used and purchased" — [StopBlaBlaCam, 15 Jan 2020](https://www.stopblablacam.com/culture-and-society/1501-3223-no-the-xaf25-000-distributed-to-musicians-on-dec-31-is-not-copyright-royalties)
- Dec 2018 (older): a search snippet says criteria for the 154 million FCFA distribution to about 1,500 artists included the artist's "degré de productivité". I did not fetch the full article — [Camer.be](https://camer.be/culture/musique/cameroun-droits-dauteurs-154-millions-pour-les-artistes-cameroon.html)
- Public venues get no usage data. Cameroon Tribune (Jan 2022) wrote there were "no modern techniques to track use of artists' works" and no coercive mechanism against non-payers — [Cameroon Tribune, 27 Jan 2022](https://www.cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/45287/fr.html/repartition-droits-dauteur-pourquoi-on-tourne-en-rond)
- Bar and lounge money since 2026 is collected as a 12 FCFA levy per crate of beer, deducted at source by brewers (SABC) and distributors (Groupe DOVV). It is a volume levy, not tied to which music is played, so it gives no per-work usage data — [Cameroon Tribune, 30 Jul 2026](https://www.cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/79271/en.html/copyrights-windfall-sonacam-secures-landmark-100-million-fcfa)
- The SONACAM website (sonacam.org, © 2026) states the principle of distributing "en fonction de l'utilisation réelle", not on notoriety. It describes a *planned* distribution rulebook based on sums collected, usage factors, work coefficients, age of fixations and traditional cultural expressions. It cites a CISAC guideline of management fees "idéalement entre 25 et 30%" and claims "80%" is redistributed. Authenticity caveat: the site sits on a third-party builder, uses inconsistent email domains (onacam.cm vs sonacam.org) and makes unsourced claims — [sonacam.org](https://www.sonacam.org/)
- The legal base is Law No. 2000/011 of 19 Dec 2000 on copyright and neighbouring rights — [OAPI PDF](https://oapi.int/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Loi_19decembre_2000Cameroun.pdf)

### Inferences
- Distribution is effectively membership-based and discretionary (flat sums or tiers decided by the Board), not data-driven. Artists' complaints that widely broadcast works are omitted (e.g. Lady Ponce, Q3) fit this.
- The beer-crate levy improves *collection* but makes per-work *attribution* even weaker, because no playlist is attached to the money.

### Gaps
- No official SONACAM règlement de répartition, percentage for administration costs, social/cultural fund share, or author/composer/publisher split was found. Neither SONACAM's site nor the press publishes them.
- I could not confirm whether CRTV or radio cue sheets (relevés de diffusion) are actually processed, or whether any sampling (sondage) of bars or nightclubs exists. Nothing found suggests either does.
- A "40,000 F" complaint threshold appeared in a search snippet attributed to Cameroon Tribune. I could not verify it in the fetched text, so treat it as unverified.
- A third-party blog (muzisecur.fr) claims SONACAM uses coefficients and recording seniority. It is a secondary, unverified source and not used here.

## Q2. Dates and amounts of recent distributions

### Takeaway
Distributions are irregular and small. The pool is roughly 35 to 154 million FCFA per round, paid to 1,379 to 2,040 beneficiaries out of about 3,000 to 6,700 claimed members. Individual payments have ranged from 2,000 to 300,000 FCFA.

- The 2022 figures conflict: 237online gives 130 M for March 2022, while Cameroon Tribune gives 110 M for "2022". They may be different rounds.

The article also mentions 850 million (currency unstated) from CRTV for repertoire clean-up, which I could not verify — [Saimondy](https://www.saimondy.com/2024/05/12/repartition-avril-sonacam-voici-liste-beneficiaires/)
- Payment is made at Express Union Finance agencies, on presentation of an ID card and a "numéro de mandat" — [Cameroun24](https://cameroun24.net/article/70951-SONACAM___debut_des_paiements_de_la_repartition_du.html)
- July 2026: 100 M FCFA recovered through the beer levy, with a distribution promised "in the weeks ahead" to help with school fees. No formula was given — [Cameroon Tribune, 30 Jul 2026](https://www.cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/79271/en.html/copyrights-windfall-sonacam-secures-landmark-100-million-fcfa)
- Membership figures conflict: "about 6,700 affiliated artists", with about 1,000 works still awaiting the identification commission ([Cameroon Tribune 2023](https://cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/58312/fr.html/details_2)), against "about 3,000 members" receiving "about 350 M FCFA/year" per the chair ([AllAfrica 2026](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202609230156.html)). The 350 M/yr figure does not fit the pool sizes above. It may count all OGCs or include social aid, so flag it as unreliable.
- Older context: in Aug 2022 the collective management bodies together distributed 340 M FCFA to 685 rights holders, with amounts from 5 M down to under 5,000 FCFA — [AllAfrica 2026](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202609230156.html)
- About 40 artists received social aid for health care in 2023 — [Cameroon Tribune 2023](https://cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/58312/fr.html/details_2)

### Inferences
- Only about 20 to 30% of claimed members are paid in a given round, and the average is tens of thousands of FCFA.
- The Board promised quarterly distributions in Dec 2020 and has not delivered them.

### Gaps
- No total or per-artist amounts for April 2024. No confirmed outcome of the post-July 2026 beer-levy distribution as of Nov 2026.
- No "50,000 FCFA minimum" rule was found.

## Q3. Controversies, court cases, government interventions and the CMC/SOCAM/SONACAM saga

### Takeaway
Music collective management in Cameroon has been in near-permanent crisis since 2005. The CMC's approval was withdrawn (2008) and the Supreme Court reversed that (2009). The CMC and SOCAM coexisted as rivals until a state-imposed merger in 2017 created SONACAM. Since then there have been a banned general assembly (2022), a court-appointed provisional administration that was contested on appeal (2022), CCOGC sanctions (2023), stalled arrears recovery, and steady artist anger over tiny, opaque payouts (2019 to 2026).

### Cited Findings
**CMC / SOCAM era (older)**
- The CMC was founded on 15 Sep 2003, with Manu Dibango as first board chair. He resigned in Feb 2005 and Sam Mbende was elected in April 2005 — [Wikipedia FR: Cameroon Music Corporation](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cameroon_Music_Corporation)
- On 12 May 2008, Culture Minister Ama Tutu Muna withdrew the CMC's agrément, alleging mismanagement and a 567 M FCFA liability. SOCAM, led by Odile Ngaska, got its agrément on 3 Jul 2008 — [Wikipedia FR](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cameroon_Music_Corporation); [Cameroon-Info.net](http://www.cameroon-info.net/article/droit-dauteur-la-socam-a-son-agrement-113181.html)
- Rulings N°02 and 03/P/CA/CS of the Supreme Court on 9 Sep 2009 reportedly rehabilitated the CMC. Other accounts say the Mbende appeal was rejected, so the sources conflict — [Wikipedia FR](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cameroon_Music_Corporation); [StopBlaBlaCam](https://www.stopblablacam.com/economie-et-politique/1404-580-la-socam-societe-en-charge-du-droit-d-auteur-des-musiciens-serait-elle-illegale)
- CPMC: Edmond VII Mballa Elanga was named president of the Commission permanente de médiation et de contrôle in Aug 2015 and told to clean up the sector. Interventions by the MINAC and the CPMC in SOCAM's affairs were criticised — [Camer.be](https://www.camer.be/amp/44955/1:6/musique); [Camer.be "Le PM veut y voir clair"](https://www.camer.be/amp/41974/1:6/cameroun-droits-dauteur-le-pm-veut-y-voir-clair-cameroon.html). The CPMC was later replaced in practice by the CCOGC (Commission de contrôle des OGC). The date and instrument of that change were not found.
- 2017: on the Presidency's instructions, the CMC signed a merger with SOCAM. SONACAM held its constitutive general assembly on 9 Sep 2017 and was approved by arrêté N°0012/MINAC on 21 Dec 2017 — [Wikipedia FR](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cameroon_Music_Corporation); [sonacam.org](https://www.sonacam.org/). Cameroon Tribune counts four names in about 20 years (CMC, SOCAM, SOCACIM, SONACAM), driven by "querelles de personnes et des intérêts" — [Cameroon Tribune, 9 Mar 2023](https://cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/55760/fr.html/droit-dauteur-le-grand-gachis)
- The CMC was excluded from CISAC in 2023 — [Wikipedia FR](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cameroon_Music_Corporation). A later court found ex-CMC/SOCAM rights-holders' claims inadmissible for lack of mandate and agrément, and said SOCAM's agrément had expired in 2013 (search summary, not fetched) — [Camer.be](https://www.camer.be/65650/1:6/cameroun-affaire-cmc-socam-c-minac-la-justice-dasavoue-le-collectif-de-fusion-cameroon.html)

**SONACAM era**
- Roméo Dika: no source found on his role in this research.
- Ateh Bazore was elected board chair on 12 Dec 2020, promising quarterly distributions instead of roughly every nine months. That goal was not met — [StopBlaBlaCam](https://www.stopblablacam.com/societe/1404-8624-sonacam-une-assemblee-generale-interdite-pour-risque-de-troubles-graves-a-l-ordre-public); [237online](https://www.237online.com/droit-dauteurs-la-sonacam-a-la-croisee-des-chemins/). He was later re-elected with 733 votes (>93%) for five years, date unconfirmed — [Lebledparle](https://www.lebledparle.com/culture-ateh-bazore-reconduit-a-la-presidence-de-la-sonacam/)
- The ORSAR operation to recover 2005 to 2017 arrears was launched by the CCOGC in Dec 2020 with a 2 billion FCFA target. It stalled because users refused to pay while the CMC/SOCAM handover was incomplete. In Feb 2022 The Music Club and the Synamurca union demanded an audit of it. SONACAM says the CMC still collects in the field despite its 2019 Supreme Court dissolution — [StopBlaBlaCam](https://www.stopblablacam.com/societe/1404-8624-sonacam-une-assemblee-generale-interdite-pour-risque-de-troubles-graves-a-l-ordre-public)
- Nov 2021: artists protested at UCB brewery in Douala, demanding royalties — same source.
- 19 Jan 2022: Minister Bidoung Mkpatt summoned the five OGCs. None had distributed regularly in the previous two years — [Cameroon Tribune, 27 Jan 2022](https://www.cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/45287/fr.html/repartition-droits-dauteur-pourquoi-on-tourne-en-rond); see also [Cameroon Tribune "rappel à l'ordre"](https://www.cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/45038/en.html/droits-dauteur-le-rappel-lordre-du-minac)
- Feb 2021: a ministerial order reassigned collection portfolios. Musicians alleged the CCOGC had given discothèques to the neighbouring-rights body SCDV, which the CCOGC denied — [Touwawen, 15 Mar 2021](https://touwawen.com/2021/03/15/gestion-des-droits-dauteur-une-bataille-de-portefeuille-inutile/fabrice/)
- 14 Apr 2022: the sub-prefect of Yaoundé 3 banned the SONACAM general assembly for "risque de troubles graves à l'ordre public" amid allegations of financial misconduct — [StopBlaBlaCam](https://www.stopblablacam.com/societe/1404-8624-sonacam-une-assemblee-generale-interdite-pour-risque-de-troubles-graves-a-l-ordre-public)
- 30 May 2022: the Tribunal de grande instance du Mfoundi (case brought by artists' association ADANG against the MINAC, the CCOGC and the SONACAM board) appointed five provisional administrators chaired by Esso Essomba. The MINAC and the board appealed to the Cour d'appel du Centre and obtained a stay. The outcome was not found — [Cameroon Tribune, 5 Jul 2022](https://cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/49423/fr.html/details_2)
- 26 Apr 2022: Lady Ponce was billed 400,000 FCFA for her cabaret and said she was again absent from distribution lists despite heavy airplay — [237online](https://www.237online.com/droit-dauteurs-la-sonacam-a-la-croisee-des-chemins/)
- Feb/Mar 2023: the CCOGC imposed "une série de sanctions" on SONACAM musicians. Details are paywalled — [Cameroon Tribune, 9 Mar 2023](https://cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/55760/fr.html/droit-dauteur-le-grand-gachis)
- Oct 2025 to Sep 2026 backlash: musicians called the 27,000 FCFA "aumône" and "insulte". DJ Lexus protested. Georges Dickson denounced SONACAM's "manque de transparence". Prince Aimé said he never received a franc for "Viviane". Henri Lotin posted a 2,000 FCFA July 2026 payment: "Vous voulez que ce pays soit pris au sérieux comment ?" SONACAM gave no public explanation — [AllAfrica/Camer.be, 22 Sep 2026](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202609230156.html); [Mimi Mefo Info](https://mimimefoinfos.com/la-sonacam-indigne-les-artistes-camerounais-avec-27-000-fcfa-davance-de-droits-dauteur/) (page did not load; title only)
- July 2026: the chair threatened that bars not paying would be sealed — [Cameroon Tribune, 30 Jul 2026](https://www.cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/79271/en.html/copyrights-windfall-sonacam-secures-landmark-100-million-fcfa)

### Inferences
- Disputes have shifted from rival-society legitimacy fights (2005 to 2019) to distribution fairness and transparency (2019 to 2026). The recurring root causes are weak collection, no usage data, and unpublished rules.

### Gaps
- No independent financial audit of SONACAM was found, and no published accounts or admin-cost ratio.
- I found no MINAC withdrawal or suspension of SONACAM's agrément in 2023 to 2026.
- The outcome of the 2022 provisional-administration appeal was not found.
- Roméo Dika's role was not researched successfully.

## Q4. Stated plans to modernise distribution with technology

### Takeaway
SONACAM's website describes a WIPOCONNECT-based system and planned monitoring: BMAT-type broadcast logs, YouTube CMS, Boomplay and Audiomack reports, and a mobile app for field licensing. The press reports no deployed monitoring as of 2026. The 2026 innovation was collection at source through beer distributors, not usage tracking.

### Cited Findings
- WIPO agreement signed 7 Jul 2021 for WIPOCONNECT, with modules for IP management, documentation, licensing/collection and distribution, using ISWC/ISRC/IPI identifiers. Planned: radio and TV logs (BMAT, Digital Media Service), platform reports (YouTube CMS, Facebook Rights Manager, Boomplay, Audiomack), a field app (photo, GPS, usage type) and a member portal "planned" — [sonacam.org](https://www.sonacam.org/) (authenticity caveat in Q1)
- 2022: talks planned with WIPO, CAPASSO (South Africa) and Spain's "Unisson" on foreign rights and reciprocity. An identification commission was updating member files — [237online](https://www.237online.com/droit-dauteurs-la-sonacam-a-la-croisee-des-chemins/)
- 2023: a joint MINAC and Commerce decision on collection at source from drinking establishments, plus planned collection from mobile operators — [Cameroon Tribune 2023](https://cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/58312/fr.html/details_2). This was implemented as the 12 FCFA per crate levy in 2026, with no tech monitoring described — [Cameroon Tribune 2026](https://www.cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/79271/en.html/copyrights-windfall-sonacam-secures-landmark-100-million-fcfa)
- Jan 2022: Cameroon Tribune noted the absence of modern tracking techniques — [Cameroon Tribune](https://www.cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/45287/fr.html/repartition-droits-dauteur-pourquoi-on-tourne-en-rond)

### Inferences
- There is a clear gap between stated intent (usage-based, WIPOCONNECT, digital logs) and practice (flat advances). Usage-data tooling (venue playlists, broadcast monitoring) would answer SONACAM's own stated goal and the MINAC's 2019 stance that payments should track use.

### Gaps
- No evidence that WIPOCONNECT is live for distribution, or that any broadcast-monitoring contract (e.g. BMAT) has been signed.
- No MINAC digitisation plan for 2024 to 2026 was found.
