How you get paid

Bars pay a copyright levy on the drinks they buy. It is collected for SONACAM and the other rights societies.

Until now that money had no playlist, so it was shared with flat advances. Fair Play meters record which songs the bars actually play.

Your share is your songs' counted plays divided by all counted plays. SONACAM applies its rules and pays you.

Shares here are indicative until SONACAM closes the period. Repeats of the same song in a short time count less, so looping a song in one bar does not inflate it.
