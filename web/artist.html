How you get paid.

1

Bars pay a copyright levy on the drinks they buy.

2

A meter in each bar recognises the songs. No sound leaves the bar.

3

Your share is your counted plays divided by all counted plays.

4

Your rights society applies its rules and pays you.

Shares are indicative until the period is closed. Repeats of the same song in a short time count less, so looping a song in one bar does not inflate it.

Fair Play is a prototype built at the CIMFEST Hackathon 2026.

Times are Cameroon time.
