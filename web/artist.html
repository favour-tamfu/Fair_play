How you get paid

  1. Bars pay a copyright levy on the drinks they buy. It is collected for SONACAM and the other rights societies.
  2. A Fair Play meter in each bar recognises the songs played. No sound leaves the bar.
  3. Your share is your songs' counted plays divided by all counted plays. Repeats of the same song in a short time count less.
  4. SONACAM applies its distribution rules and pays you.

Shares are indicative until SONACAM closes the period.
