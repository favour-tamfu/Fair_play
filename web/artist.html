    How the share works.

    1

    Bars pay SONACAM a copyright levy on the drinks they buy.

    2

    A meter in each bar recognises the songs. No sound leaves the bar.

    3

    A song's share is its counted plays divided by all counted plays. An artist's share is the sum of their songs' shares.

    4

    SONACAM shares the levy by these shares and pays its members under its own rules.

    Repeats of the same song in a short time count less, so looping a song in one bar does not inflate it. Unusual patterns are flagged for SONACAM to review.

    Fair Play is a prototype built at the CIMFEST Hackathon 2026.

    Times are Cameroon time.
